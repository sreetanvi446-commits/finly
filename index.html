Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Who we are

Finly is an AI-powered financial education assistant built for Indians. We help users understand taxes, investing, trading, and personal finance. Finly does not provide professional financial or legal advice.

What information we collect

  • Name and email address when you sign in with Google or create an account
  • Profile photo from Google (if you sign in with Google)
  • Questions you ask Finly (used to provide answers, not stored permanently)
  • Usage data such as number of questions asked

How we use your information

  • To personalise your experience and greet you by name
  • To track your daily question limit (free plan)
  • To process payments if you upgrade to Pro via Razorpay
  • To improve Finly's responses and features

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone
  • We do not share your data with third parties except Razorpay for payment processing and Firebase for authentication
  • We do not store your financial details or tax information

Third party services

Finly uses Firebase (Google) for authentication, Razorpay for payments, Groq for AI responses, and Alpha Vantage for market data. Each of these services has their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. You can also sign out at any time to stop using the service.

Contact

For any privacy concerns email us at finlyapp.in@gmail.com

Finly is an educational tool. Nothing on Finly constitutes professional financial, tax, or legal advice. Always consult a registered CA or SEBI advisor for actual financial decisions.
