FAQ

Frequently asked
questions 🤔

What is Finly and how does it work?+
Finly is a free AI-powered finance assistant built specifically for Indians. Ask any question about taxes, investing, GST, or money — get a clear, detailed answer instantly. It's powered by advanced AI with deep knowledge of India's finance system. 🤖
Is Finly really free? What's the catch?+
Yes, Finly is genuinely free for up to 10 questions per day. No catch. We're building a Pro version with unlimited questions for a small monthly fee, but the core experience will always be free. ✅
Is my data safe? Do you store my questions?+
Your privacy is our priority. 🔒 We do not permanently store your questions or financial details. We never sell data to anyone. Your questions are processed to give you an answer — that's it.
Can I trust Finly's financial advice?+
Finly is a powerful educational tool, not a registered financial advisor. Our answers are accurate and detailed, but for complex decisions always verify with a registered CA or SEBI advisor. Finly will always tell you when that's the case. 🙏
Who built Finly?+
Finly was built by Sree Tanvi — a 16 year old founder from Bengaluru, currently pursuing Data Science and AI at IIT Madras. Built from scratch with the mission of making financial knowledge accessible to every Indian. 🚀
What topics does Finly cover?+
ITR filing, income tax slabs, old vs new tax regime, Section 80C deductions, GST, SIP and mutual funds, crypto tax (30% + 1% TDS), EMI calculations, financial health scoring, salary analysis and much more. 💰
